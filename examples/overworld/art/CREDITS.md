# Example art

`world.png`, `exterior.png`, `interior.png`, `dungeon.png`, `ship.png` are free tilesets
from itch.io, included with permission of this project's author.

**Attribution is incomplete.** Most free itch.io packs ask for credit even when they allow
redistribution, and the specific pack and author are not recorded here. If you know the
source, please add it — pack name, author, URL, and licence. Until then, treat these as
"used by permission, provenance unverified" rather than as public domain.

`tileset.png`, `cave.png`, `hero.png`, `npc.png` are generated by
`tools/pnx_placeholder.py` and are original to this project.

`npc_ice.png` and `npc_rust.png` are recolours of `npc.png`, produced by remapping its
quantised colours one-to-one. They demonstrate the `variants` feature -- one bitmap, one palette
per recolour -- and inherit `npc.png`'s status: original to this project. They are committed
rather than generated at build time because the manifest names them as inputs. They exist so the example can
be rebuilt from nothing if the tilesets above ever need removing, and so the test suite
never depends on third-party art.
